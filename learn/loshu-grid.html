What is the Lo Shu Grid?

An ancient Chinese 3×3 magic square in which every row, column and diagonal sums to 15. In numerology it acts as a personal map: digits from your date of birth are placed into the grid, revealing which energies are present, repeated, or missing.

Why it matters: a present number indicates a natural strength; a missing number flags an area to develop. Lines of three present numbers form "arrows" of strength; lines of three missing numbers form "arrows of absence" worth addressing.

Day, month and all four year digits are used.
Method & conventions used

Digits placed in the grid: every digit of your day, month and four-digit year. Zeros are skipped (the Lo Shu Grid has no zero cell).

Added energies: per the common Vedic-numerology convention, your Mulank (birth day reduced) and Bhagyank (full DOB reduced) are added to the grid as well, enriching the reading.

Repetitions matter: if a digit appears three times, the cell shows all three. Each occurrence amplifies that energy.

Plane status: Full = all three digits of the line are present; Partial = one or two are present; Empty = none are present (a "missing arrow" worth working on).
