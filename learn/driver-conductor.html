Driver & Conductor

Mulank · Bhagyank · Vedic Numerology

What are Driver and Conductor?

Two of the most fundamental personal numbers in Vedic numerology, both derived purely from your date of birth.

Mulank (Driver / Birth Number) — the single-digit reduction of your day of birth. Reflects your outer personality, instincts and natural drive.

Bhagyank (Conductor / Destiny Number) — the single-digit reduction of your full date of birth (day + month + year). Reflects your life path, opportunities and longer-arc purpose.

Why both: the Driver shows how you operate day-to-day; the Conductor shows where life tends to lead you. When they harmonise, action and direction reinforce each other. When they conflict, life often feels like work pulling against instinct.

Enter the calendar date — no time-zone or place needed.
Method & formula

Mulank: take only your birth day. If it is two digits, add them together until you get a single digit (1–9). Example: 27 → 2+7 = 9.

Bhagyank: add every digit of your full date of birth (dd + mm + all four year digits). Reduce the sum to a single digit. Example: 27/03/1990 → 2+7+0+3+1+9+9+0 = 31 → 3+1 = 4.

Each single digit is governed by one of the nine Vedic planets (Navagraha): 1=Sun, 2=Moon, 3=Jupiter, 4=Rahu, 5=Mercury, 6=Venus, 7=Ketu, 8=Saturn, 9=Mars.
