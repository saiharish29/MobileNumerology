What this is

A small collection of standalone calculators, each focused on one classical concept drawn from Vedic numerology and Feng Shui. Every formula and every table here is sourced from public references — nothing is invented for effect.

Use these alongside the parent Mobile Numerology Analyzer for a fuller picture, or on their own to learn what each concept actually says.

Principles we follow
  • Sourced, not invented. Every rule, formula and direction table cites at least one public reference. Where classical systems disagree (e.g. Cheiro vs. Pythagorean on number 19), we name both and pick a side openly.
  • Educational framing. Each calculator answers: what is this, why does it matter, how was it calculated, common misconceptions.
  • Accessibility first. Keyboard navigation, ARIA labels, sufficient colour contrast, respects prefers-reduced-motion.
  • No tracking, no backend. All calculation runs in your browser. Nothing leaves your device.
Verification
